# SADIE II head-related impulse responses (Neumann KU100)

The 360 files here are the horizontal-plane measurements (elevation 0, every
whole degree of azimuth, 48 kHz / 24 bit) of subject D1, the Neumann KU100
dummy head, from the SADIE II Database, unmodified.

- Source: `D1/D1_HRIR_WAV/48K_24bit/` in `D1.zip`, https://zenodo.org/records/10886409
- Project page: https://www.york.ac.uk/sadie-project/database.html
- Copyright 2018 University of York. Licensed under the Apache License,
  Version 2.0: http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
- Reference: C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. Murphy and G. Kearney, "A Perceptual
  Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the
  SADIE II Database", Applied Sciences 8(11), 2018. doi:10.3390/app8112029

Azimuth counts anti-clockwise: `azi_30,0` is 30 degrees to the left.
